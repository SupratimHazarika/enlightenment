Measuring a hand instead of guessing

Classic palmistry texts call palms and fingers "long" or "short" by eye, so two readers often disagree. Measuring makes your observation repeatable, though not your interpretation.

Three measurements

  • P, palm length: from the top wrist crease to the crease at the base of the middle finger.
  • B, palm breadth across the knuckles, excluding the thumb.
  • F, middle-finger length from its base crease to the tip.

Worked example

Meera measures P = 10.2 cm, B = 8.5 cm and F = 7.9 cm. P/B = 10.2 ÷ 8.5 = 1.20, so her palm is oblong under the course convention (oblong if P/B > 1.10). F/P = 7.9 ÷ 10.2 = 0.7745…, which rounds directly to 0.77. That falls between 0.75 and 0.85, so her fingers count as medium.

These cut-offs are the course's own teaching thresholds. No historical source specifies them, and no research shows they mean anything about personality. Describe borderline values as borderline.
