Independent introduction

Access Bars: An Independent Introduction

What it is, what to expect, and how to judge the claims, written independently and with sources.

2 chapters · 19 pages · reflection and evaluation worksheets · quiz with explained answers · glossary and referenced sources

Tradition: A modern practice dated by its own organisation to the 1990s. Not Vedic, not ancient.

What you will be able to do

  • Describe Access Bars using the organisation's own public description
  • Say who developed it and why published dates differ
  • Tell apart what is claimed from what has been shown
  • Set out consent, comfort and safety boundaries before booking a session
  • Read the published studies critically (the largest we read had 42 participants, and both groups improved)
  • Spot red flags and know when to see a licensed professional

Prerequisites

None.

Scope and limits

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Access Consciousness. Not authorised training or certification, and it does not teach the technique (no point maps or sequences). Not a treatment for any condition.
