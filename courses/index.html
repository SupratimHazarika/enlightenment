Courses

Three full beginner courses and one independent introduction. Each one teaches the method, its sources and its limits.

43 pages · 13 lessons

Reading Tarot: A Practical Course in the 78 Cards

Learn to read every card from its picture, not from memorised keywords, using Pamela Colman Smith's public-domain 1909 artwork and A. E. Waite's own 1911 notes.

European card tradition (15th-century Italy; divination documented from the 18th century). Not Vedic, not Egyptian.

44 pages · 12 lessons

Reading the Hand: A Complete Beginner's Course in Palmistry Traditions

A workbook, not a dictionary of meanings: learn the classic vocabulary, where the traditions agree and disagree, and how to give a careful, hedged reading.

Indian Samudrika (Hasta Samudrika) tradition alongside European chiromancy and the 19th-century revival, presented as cultural history.

50 pages · 12 lessons

Jyotish: A Beginner's Working Atlas of Indian Sidereal Astrology

Measure the sky, name the divisions, draw the chart, then evaluate the claims. A coordinate model comes before any story.

Indian sidereal astrology (Jyotish). Distinct from Western tropical astrology; the arithmetic is taught separately from traditional interpretation.

19 pages · 2 chapters

Access Bars: An Independent Introduction

What it is, what to expect, and how to judge the claims, written independently and with sources.

A modern practice dated by its own organisation to the 1990s. Not Vedic, not ancient.
