Jyotish: A Beginner's Working Atlas of Indian Sidereal Astrology

Measure the sky, name the divisions, draw the chart, then evaluate the claims. A coordinate model comes before any story.

12 lessons · 50 pages · 46 original diagrams · script-checked calculations · worked fictional chart · glossary, answers and study path

Tradition: Indian sidereal astrology (Jyotish). Distinct from Western tropical astrology; the arithmetic is taught separately from traditional interpretation.

What you will be able to do

  • Convert a supplied longitude between tropical and sidereal frames (Lahiri-labelled), without confusing decimal degrees with minutes
  • Identify rashi, nakshatra (27 × 13°20′) and pada, and see why sign and nakshatra boundaries coincide every 120°
  • Use the twelve rashis, nine grahas and whole-sign houses
  • Place a fictional chart in North and South Indian formats
  • Calculate a first Vimshottari balance and a sub-period length, stating the year convention
  • Read Varahamihira's Brihat Jataka (Aiyar's 1905 English edition) critically
  • Discuss traditional symbolism without diagnosing, discriminating or promising results

Prerequisites

Comfort with basic arithmetic (degrees, minutes, division). No birth data needed; the course supplies fictional examples.

Scope and limits

Cultural study and entertainment. No death, disease, fertility or marriage verdicts and no paid remedies. Not yet reviewed by a Jyotish or Sanskrit specialist.
