Reading the Hand: A Complete Beginner's Course in Palmistry Traditions

A workbook, not a dictionary of meanings: learn the classic vocabulary, where the traditions agree and disagree, and how to give a careful, hedged reading.

12 lessons · 44 pages · 23 original hand diagrams · fingerprint-pattern practical · three quizzes with 30 explained answers

Tradition: Indian Samudrika (Hasta Samudrika) tradition alongside European chiromancy and the 19th-century revival, presented as cultural history.

What you will be able to do

  • Place the Indian Samudrika tradition, European chiromancy texts and the 19th-century revival on a timeline
  • Explain what science does and does not support, and open a reading with consent and a disclaimer
  • Name hand regions, creases, phalanges and mounts in anatomical and traditional terms
  • Classify a hand in Cheiro's seven-type system and measure palm and finger proportions
  • Locate the nine traditional mounts and trace the life, head, heart, fate and minor lines
  • Recognise twelve classic marks and explain why "fatal" readings are not used
  • Summarise what Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita says about hands, as text, not as fact
  • Run a complete seven-step reading and write a short hedged summary

Prerequisites

None. A ruler, a notebook and good light. Practise on your own hand first.

Scope and limits

Palmistry has not been shown to predict personality, health, lifespan, wealth or events. No health, lifespan or marriage claims are made.
