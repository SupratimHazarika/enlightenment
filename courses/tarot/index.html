Reading Tarot: A Practical Course in the 78 Cards

Learn to read every card from its picture, not from memorised keywords, using Pamela Colman Smith's public-domain 1909 artwork and A. E. Waite's own 1911 notes.

13 lessons · 43 pages · all 78 cards covered one by one · quizzes with explained answers after Lessons 3, 9 and 12 · worksheets and worked readings

Tradition: European card tradition (15th-century Italy; divination documented from the 18th century). Not Vedic, not Egyptian.

What you will be able to do

  • Separate tarot's documented history (a 15th-century Italian card game) from popular myths
  • Name and count every part of the 78-card deck
  • Use a 5-step method to read an unfamiliar card from its scene
  • Explain all 22 Major Arcana card by card, upright and reversed
  • Interpret all 56 minor cards from their scenes, and read court cards as people, roles or approaches
  • Choose and apply one consistent reversal method
  • Lay out a 3-card spread and the Celtic Cross, following three worked readings
  • Read responsibly and keep a 30-day practice journal

Prerequisites

None. You need a Rider–Waite–Smith-style deck (or the card images in the book), a notebook and about 20–30 minutes a day. A realistic pace is two lessons a week, about six to seven weeks.

Scope and limits

For reflection, conversation and entertainment. It does not predict events, diagnose health or give legal or financial advice.
