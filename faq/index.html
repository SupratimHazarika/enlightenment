Frequently asked questions

Are these courses "Vedic"?

Only the Jyotish course covers an Indian tradition, and it notes that "Vedic astrology" is a modern label. Not every rule in it occurs in the Vedas. Tarot is a European card tradition, and Access Bars is a modern practice from the 1990s.

Will this predict my future or fix a problem?

No. The courses teach traditions, methods and their limits. They make no predictions, guarantees, diagnoses or cures.

What exactly do I get?

PDF workbooks: Tarot (43 pages, 13 lessons), Palmistry (44 pages, 12 lessons), Jyotish (50 pages, 12 lessons) and the Access Bars independent introduction (19 pages). Each has objectives, worked examples, worksheets and quizzes with explained answers.

Why is Access Bars only an introduction?

We are not authorised to teach the technique. The guide explains what it is, the evidence and how to choose wisely. It is not training or certification.
