What makes these different

Method before meaning

Jyotish starts with the sky map and arithmetic, such as 27 nakshatras of 13°20′. Interpretation comes only after that, and it is labelled as tradition.

Real sources, named

Varahamihira (Aiyar's 1905 translation), Waite's 1911 notes and Cheiro's system, with each source's access limits stated.

Practice, not just reading

Worked examples, worksheets and quizzes whose answers explain the reasoning.

No false promises

No predictions, cures, guaranteed results, fake reviews or countdown timers.
